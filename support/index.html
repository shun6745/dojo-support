DOJO Support

Welcome to DOJO, a real estate property discovery app operated by HOMECANVASR NY INC.

We’re here to help with technical issues, feedback, and questions about using the DOJO app.

Contact Support

For technical support, bug reports, feedback, or feature requests, please contact us at:

jun@bkdrealestate.com

When contacting support, please include:

  • Your device model
  • Your iOS version
  • The version of the DOJO app, if available
  • The screen or feature where the issue occurred
  • A brief description of the problem
  • The steps that caused the issue
  • A screenshot, if helpful

Please do not include sensitive personal information, financial information, passwords, credit information, or government identification in a support request.

Property Questions

If you have a question about a specific property, please use the Contact button on the property’s detail page.

Depending on the property, your device may open your email application with relevant property information included so you can contact the appropriate real estate professional directly.

Property availability, pricing, showing schedules, application requirements, and other property-specific information are provided by the applicable listing or real estate professional.

Feedback & Feature Requests

We welcome your feedback.

If you have an idea for improving DOJO or would like to request a feature, please contact us at:

jun@bkdrealestate.com

We review feedback and feature requests as part of our ongoing development of the App.

Privacy

DOJO does not require users to create an account to browse properties.

DOJO does not ask users to provide income, credit scores, employment status, student status, government identification, or rental application information simply to use the App.

For more information about how information is handled, please review our Privacy Policy.
