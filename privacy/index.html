Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 8, 2026

DOJO is a real estate property discovery application operated by HOMECANVASR NY INC ("HOMECANVASR," "we," "us," or "our").

We respect your privacy and are committed to being transparent about how information is handled when you use the DOJO application ("App").

This Privacy Policy explains what information DOJO collects, how information may be used, and the choices available to you.

Information We Collect

DOJO is designed to allow users to browse and explore real estate properties without creating an account.

Property Browsing

You may browse property listings, photographs, descriptions, pricing information, and other property-related information without registering for an account.

Your property searches or browsing activity may be processed locally on your device to provide features of the App.

Information You Voluntarily Provide

If you choose to contact a real estate agent, broker, property owner, or another third party through the App, information that you voluntarily provide through that communication may be shared with the recipient.

For example, if you choose to send an email or message to an agent, the information contained in that communication will be handled by the applicable communication service and recipient.

DOJO does not require you to submit personal information merely to browse properties.

Information Collected Automatically

Depending on the version of the App and the services used to operate it, certain technical information may be processed automatically, such as:

  • Device type
  • Operating system
  • App version
  • Basic diagnostic information
  • Crash or error information
  • IP address or other technical information processed by network services

The specific information collected depends on the technical services and third-party software integrated into the App.

We do not use this information to create a real estate applicant profile containing your income, credit score, employment status, or other financial qualifications.

Location Information

DOJO does not require your precise location to browse properties.

If a future version of the App requests access to location services, DOJO will request permission through Apple’s applicable system controls and will explain the purpose of the requested access.

You can control location permissions through your device settings.

Cookies and Tracking Technologies

DOJO does not require cookies or tracking technologies for users to browse properties.

If analytics, crash-reporting, advertising, or other third-party technologies are added to the App in the future, this Privacy Policy and our App Store privacy disclosures will be updated as appropriate.

Where required, applicable permissions or consent mechanisms will be provided.

How We Use Information

Information handled by DOJO may be used to:

  • Provide and operate the App
  • Display and organize real estate listings
  • Improve the App’s functionality
  • Diagnose technical problems
  • Maintain App security
  • Respond to communications initiated by users
  • Prevent fraud, abuse, or unauthorized activity
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations

We do not use DOJO to evaluate users based on income, credit score, employment status, student status, or other financial qualifications.

Sharing of Information

HOMECANVASR NY INC does not sell your personal information.

Information may be shared when necessary to provide a feature that you intentionally use.

For example, if you choose to contact a real estate professional through the App, information included in your communication may be transmitted to that person or business so they can respond to you.

We may also disclose information:

  • To service providers that help operate the App;
  • When necessary to protect the security or integrity of the App;
  • When required by law, regulation, court order, or legal process; or
  • When reasonably necessary to protect the rights, property, or safety of HOMECANVASR NY INC, our users, or others.

Third-party providers may have their own privacy policies governing information they receive.

Third-Party Services and Links

DOJO may contain links to third-party websites, property listing services, brokerage websites, email services, or other external services.

When you leave DOJO and visit a third-party website or service, that third party’s privacy policy and terms may apply.

HOMECANVASR NY INC is not responsible for the privacy practices, security, or content of third-party websites or services.

Data Retention

Because DOJO does not require user accounts or collect financial qualification information, we generally do not maintain a user profile containing information such as income, credit score, employment status, or rental application documents.

Information voluntarily provided through communications or information processed by third-party services may be retained according to the applicable service provider’s policies and applicable legal requirements.

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, to provide our services, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, maintain security, or comply with legal obligations.

Data Security

We take reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures designed to protect information handled by DOJO from unauthorized access, use, alteration, or disclosure.

However, no method of transmitting or storing information over the Internet or through electronic systems can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Children’s Privacy

DOJO is not directed specifically toward children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly request or collect personal information from children under 13.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so that we can review the situation and take appropriate action.

Your Privacy Choices

You may choose not to provide personal information to DOJO.

You can also control certain device permissions through Apple’s device settings, including permissions relating to location or other protected device resources.

If you voluntarily contact a third party through DOJO, that communication may be subject to the third party’s own privacy practices.

California and Other U.S. Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live and applicable law, you may have certain rights regarding personal information, including rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information.

Because DOJO is designed to operate without requiring user accounts or collecting financial qualification information, the amount and type of personal information associated with an individual user may be limited.

To make a privacy-related request, please contact us using the information below.

New York Privacy and Security

HOMECANVASR NY INC operates in New York and takes reasonable measures to protect information handled through its services.

Where applicable, we maintain reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards appropriate to the information we maintain.

We also aim to ensure that statements about our privacy and data practices accurately reflect how DOJO operates.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to DOJO, our services, applicable laws, or our privacy practices.

When we make material changes, we may update the Effective Date shown at the top of this Privacy Policy and provide additional notice where appropriate.

You should periodically review this Privacy Policy for the most current information.
