REPLACE_LEGAL_ENTITY_NAME ("we," "us," or "our") provides this Privacy Policy to explain how information is handled in the DOJO property catalogue app.

Information handled by the app

DOJO does not provide user accounts or use advertising, behavioral tracking, analytics, location data, contacts, photos, the camera, or the microphone.

Search preferences and saved properties are stored locally on your device and are not sent to our servers. You can remove saved information within the app, and it is also removed from the device when you uninstall the app.

Retrieving property information

The app connects to our public server to display property information and images. Cloudflare, our infrastructure provider, may temporarily process IP addresses and request information to deliver the service and maintain its security. We do not use this information for advertising or user tracking.

Property enquiries

The contact action on a property page opens your device's email app. Your message is sent directly from your chosen email service to the listing agent and does not pass through DOJO or our servers. After it is sent, the receiving business handles the information under its own policies.

Changes to this policy

We may update this page when the app's features or information practices change. We will provide notice in the app or on this page if a change is significant.
